El Nido felt like stepping into another world: dramatic karst cliffs rising from the sea, clear lagoons glowing in brilliant shades of blue, and nights that drifted by on warm island air.

We spent our days swimming in untouched coves, kayaking between jagged limestone walls, and chasing golden hour along the shoreline. Every corner of the island seemed designed for a postcard.
